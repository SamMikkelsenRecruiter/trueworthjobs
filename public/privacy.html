8. Cookies

We use Cookiebot to manage cookie consent. The first time you visit, you'll see a banner asking your consent for non-essential cookies. You can change your consent at any time using the cookie banner.

Cookies that may be set on this website:

  • Essential / strictly necessary cookies — set by our hosting provider (Netlify) for site security and by Cookiebot to remember your consent preferences. These do not require consent under PECR and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics cookies (Google Analytics 4) — only set if you accept "Statistics" cookies via the consent banner. Used to understand aggregate site usage. IP addresses are anonymised and we do not run any advertising or remarketing tags.

You can also control or delete cookies via your browser settings.
